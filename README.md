**NutriLink AI**

NutriLink AI uses artificial intelligence to connect consumers, farmers, and food innovators with affordable alternative protein options through intelligent nutrition recommendations, food formulation, and agricultural insights.

**Problem:**

Nigeria faces a growing challenge of food affordability and limited access to diverse, affordable protein sources, despite having locally available protein-rich crops that remain underutilized.
Who faces it: Low- and middle-income households, individuals with nutritional needs, food entrepreneurs, and farmers who struggle with affordability, nutritional information, product innovation, and market access.
What it causes today: Poor dietary diversity, difficulty affording nutritious meals, dependence on expensive protein sources, and missed economic opportunities for farmers and food innovators. According to Nigeria's NBS household survey, approximately 2 in 3 households reported being unable to eat healthy or nutritious food because of lack of money.

**Problem:**
Limited access to affordable, nutritious, and diverse protein sources, alongside the underutilization of locally available alternative proteins.
Who faces it: Low-income households, consumers with nutritional needs, food entrepreneurs, farmers, and food suppliers.
What it causes today:
Difficulty affording nutritious meals.
Poor dietary diversity and nutritional deficiencies.
Overdependence on expensive protein sources.
Limited opportunities for farmers and food innovators.

**What it does:**

Uses AI to recommend alternative protein sources, provide nutrition insights, support protein formulation, and connect users to farmers and suppliers.
Who it is for: Consumers, individuals with nutritional needs, food entrepreneurs, farmers, food manufacturers, and suppliers.
How it solves the problem:
Helps consumers discover affordable, locally available protein alternatives.
Provides nutritional data to support informed food choices.
Helps food innovators develop and explore alternative-protein formulations.
Connects farmers and suppliers to potential buyers and food businesses.
Uses data-driven insights to support smarter agricultural and market decisions.

**INPUTS**

• Nutritional requirements

• Local protein ingredient data

• Ingredient prices and availability

• Agricultural and market data

**2. AI Model**

• Machine learning for ingredient analysis and recommendations.

• Optimization algorithms for creating protein formulations.

**USP**: Combines nutrition intelligence, alternative-protein formulation, and agricultural market connections in one platform.

**3. Outputs**
• Protein recommendations

• Nutritional insights

• Optimized ingredient combinations

• Supplier discovery

• Agricultural and market insights
